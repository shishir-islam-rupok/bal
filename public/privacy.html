PRIVACY POLICY

Your files stay with you.

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Local image processing

File Shift converts supported images in your browser. The selected image files are not uploaded to or stored on a File Shift conversion server.

Media processing

Audio and video conversion uses FFmpeg WebAssembly running in your browser. Media files are processed on your device. Large files may use your device memory and processor.

Technical information

Our hosting provider may receive normal technical request information, such as an IP address, browser type, request time, and requested resource, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. File Shift does not require an account.

Cookies and advertising

Essential technologies may be used to operate and secure the website. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, its providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize advertising according to the choices and disclosures presented on the live site.

Third-party services

The website may use hosting, font, analytics, or advertising providers. The live version of this policy must be updated with the exact providers and retention practices before those services are enabled.

Your choices

You can remove selected files, close the page, or clear site data to remove browser-held conversion data. Contact us with privacy questions through the Contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy when the product or legal requirements change. The current version is published on this page.
